Cookie Notice

Effective: [[CONFIRM: effective date]] · Last updated: August 21, 2026

This website sets no cookies. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no consent banner needed — because there is nothing to consent to. If that ever changes, this notice changes with it and a banner appears before any non-essential cookie is set.

1. The marketing website

The pages under this domain are static. They set no cookies and use no local storage. Two outbound requests are made from your browser:

RequestWhyWhat it involves
Google FontsLoads the typeface the site is set in.Google receives your IP address and the font request, per Google's privacy policy. No cookie is set by us.
Our own APIReads the published price list so the pricing section is current.A single read-only request. No cookie, no credentials, nothing identifying you is sent.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and diagnostics. That is a server log, not a cookie, and it is not used to profile visitors.

2. The MetricsPro application

The signed-in application is a different system, and it does use browser storage — but only what is strictly necessary to work:

WhatPurposeLasts
Authentication tokenKeeps you signed in between page loads. Without it, every click would require a fresh login.Until you sign out or the session expires.
Session and preference valuesRemembers the company you are acting as, the reporting period you selected, and layout choices such as a collapsed menu.Until cleared.

These are essential to providing a service you asked for, so they do not require consent under cookie rules. There is no advertising or cross-site tracking anywhere in the application.

3. Controlling storage

Your browser can block or clear site data at any time. Blocking it for the application will sign you out and prevent it from working. Blocking it for this website has no effect, because there is nothing to block.
